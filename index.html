Be a star... Learn to dance!

Dance and baton classes for ages 2½ through adults in Newark and Bear, taught by Miss Vicky since 1981.

Call to register See the classes

Fun learning is what we believe in.

Dance offers the opportunity for children to express themselves, to gain poise and better their self esteem. To keep active, make new friends and most of all to learn about themselves and the art of dance.

We strive to offer the best dance instruction in a fun, lively, contemporary atmosphere in a more personal setting. It is the smiles on their faces that we love to bring out on stage and during class.

Our classes, for ages 2½ through adults

  • BalletClassical style.
  • PointeBallet performed on the tips of the toes.
  • TapTap dance: making rhythms with your feet.
  • JazzModern style.
  • LyricalA combination of ballet, modern and jazz dancing.
  • Hip-hopFree-style, breaking, locking and popping.

Miss Vicky has taught here since 1981

Miss Vicky Vitalo Piane trained under Dottie Bounds for 11 years, from the age of 8, in all forms and levels of dance: ballet, pointe, tap, jazz and lyrical. In her early years she was a student of Sue Stroman, the award-winning choreographer of the Broadway hit The Producers. She was a ballroom instructor at Fred Astaire's for 4 years.

She added hip-hop and baton when she opened her studio in 1981. Her school is now past its 30th year, and she is teaching many of the children of her dancers. Her students have won multiple awards in national and local competitions.
